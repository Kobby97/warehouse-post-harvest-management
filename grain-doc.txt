Our team built the hardware around an ESP32 microcontroller, which serves as the core brain of our prototype. To monitor grain health, we connected a DHT22 sensor inside the silo, which continuously tracks internal temperature and humidity to detect early risks of mold or spoilage. To estimate the quantity of grain stored, we mounted an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor at the top of the silo pointing downward. It bounces ultrasonic sound waves off the grain pile to measure the distance to its surface. By subtracting that distance from the silo's total height, the ESP32 calculates the volume and fill level in real time. The ESP32 processes all this data and transmits it via REST APIs over Wi-Fi to our Spring Boot backend for dashboard visualization. Lastly, if temperature thresholds are breached or grain levels drop too low, the ESP32 automatically triggers a local Red LED and a Piezo Buzzer to provide instant hardware-level warnings.
